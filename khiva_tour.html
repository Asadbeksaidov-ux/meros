Magic of Ichan-Kala: Full Day Program

Immerse yourself in the atmosphere of the Silk Road. This curated walking tour covers all the major landmarks of the UNESCO World Heritage site Ichan-Kala.

Tour Essentials

  • Duration: Approx. 4-5 hours (Flexible Pace)
  • Meeting Point: From your hotel or location in Khiva
  • Start Time: Flexible (09:00 AM recommended)
  • Activity Level: Private Walking Tour
  • Guide Service: Experienced Licensed Guide (English or Russian)
  • Exclusions: Entrance tickets, lunch

PROGRAM

  • 1. Mukhammad Aminkhan Madrasah & Minaret: The iconic unfinished blue tower (Kalta Minor) and the largest medieval madrasah.
  • 2. Kunya Ark Palace: The ancient citadel and residence of the Khans.
  • 3. Mukhammad Rahimkhan Madrasah: A historical center of education and poetry.
  • 4. The Exhibition of Ak-Mechet Mennonites : A unique small mosque with a conical dome.
  • 5. Khiva Silk Carpet Workshop: See the traditional silk weaving process.
  • 6. Pakhlavan Makhmud Mausoleum: The sacred mausoleum with stunning turquoise tiles.
  • 7. Islam-Khodja Madrasah & Minaret: The tallest minaret in Khiva giving a skyline view.
  • 8. Juma Mosque: The unique Friday Mosque with 213 wooden columns.
  • 9. Mukhammad Amin Inaq Madrasah: One of the earliest buildings in the eastern part.
  • 10. Tash-Khovli Palace: The "Stone Palace" known for its intricate Harem and courtyards.
  • 11. Caravan Sarai: Examples of the trading heritage (Allakuli Khan).
